<b>Lalim — Privacy Policy</b>

<b>What Lalim stores</b>
• Your save files: the name you enter, your story progress, collected items and completed challenges.
• Gameplay statistics used to adjust difficulty (for example, how many times a section was retried).
• Your settings (volume, graphics, controls).

<b>Where it is stored</b>
Everything stays on this device. Lalim has no accounts, no ads, and does not send your data to the internet.

<b>Deleting your data</b>
Use Settings ▸ System ▸ Erase Save Data from the main menu, or uninstall the game.

<b>Children</b>
Lalim does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
